Grounded in Doctoral Research

FEONA's AI and learning analytics framework originates from pioneering academic research conducted in collaboration with the University of Helsinki (Faculty of Educational Sciences).

Core Research Foundation: "Predictive Analytics in Non-Formal STEM Environments: A Behavioral Machine Learning Framework for Longitudinal Career Aptitude Mapping" (Nataliya Rozhnova, Maria Rohnonen, and Anton Poppel).

By capturing stealth behavioral metrics—such as iteration rates, resource efficiency, and rule discovery latency during complex problem-solving—FEONA quantifies unmeasured cognitive growth to build objective, data-driven competency profiles.
