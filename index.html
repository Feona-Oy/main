Grounded in Doctoral Research

FEONA's AI and learning analytics framework stems from pioneering academic research at the University of Helsinki (Faculty of Educational Sciences) regarding predictive analytics and behavioral machine learning in non-formal STEM environments (Nataliya Rozhnova, Maria Rohnonen).

This framework is backed by Maria Rohnonen's doctoral research (PhD) at LUT University specializing in Digital Twins in Transport.

By capturing stealth behavioral metrics—such as iteration rates, resource efficiency, and rule discovery latency during complex problem-solving—FEONA quantifies unmeasured cognitive growth to build objective, data-driven competency profiles.
