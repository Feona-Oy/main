SkillsForge by FEONA

Every workshop becomes a step on a skills map

SkillsForge is FEONA's AI platform for STEAM and entrepreneurship learning. A workshop, like building this go-kart or a solar boat, sits on each learner's map with its level, the skills it grows and what the instructor looks for.

  • Learners see a good next step on their own map
  • Shows which skills grow, such as planning and perseverance (EntreComp)
  • Clear evaluation: what the instructor looks for, and credits when evaluated
